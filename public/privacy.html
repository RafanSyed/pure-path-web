Pure Path — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Pure Path is a personal content-filtering browser extension. This page explains what data it collects and what happens to it.

What data is collected

  • The domain and URL of websites visited
  • Search queries entered on Google and Bing
  • YouTube video URLs

Why it's collected

This data is sent to a private backend server to determine whether a site, search, or video should be allowed or blocked, based on filtering rules configured by the extension's owner.

Where it's sent

  • A private backend server controlled by the developer, where classification decisions are made and domain rules are stored.
  • OpenAI — search queries, website details, and video titles may be sent to OpenAI's API for AI-based content classification.
  • YouTube Data API (Google) — YouTube video IDs are sent to check channel and content restriction status.

Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than content filtering.

Data retention

Domains that have been classified are stored in the backend database (categorized as safe, conditionally safe, or blocked) until manually removed by the extension's owner.

Who this extension is for

Pure Path is built and used for personal, individual content-filtering purposes. It is not intended for use by the general public.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer.
